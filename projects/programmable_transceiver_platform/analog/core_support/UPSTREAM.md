# Upstream sources of the vendored core support cells

Files under `por/`, `biasgen/` and `bandgap/` are unmodified copies of the pinned upstream revisions below.
`manifest/por.sha256` (70 files), `manifest/biasgen.sha256` (75) and `manifest/bandgap.sha256` (80) hold their
SHA-256 hashes; the `verify` step of `run_core_support.sh` checks them before every run.

## Local modifications

Local changes are kept out of the vendored trees. They are patches in `patches/`, applied in name order to
scratch copies by `flow/clean_flow.sh`:

| Patch | Change |
| --- | --- |
| `biasgen-dvdd-dvss-pin-alignment.patch` | Moves the top-level `dvdd`/`dvss` metal4 pins onto the frontend supply straps. As vendored, `dvdd` lands on the ground strap. |
| `biasgen-vfb-compensation.patch` | Adds 4 × (100 × 38 µm) MIM capacitors (about 30 pF) from `vfb` to `avss` above the cell, wired on metal5. Adds `C3` (m = 4) to the `bias_generator_fe` schematic and the generated paramcell `cap_mim_2p0fF_VFBCMP.mag`. |

| Cell | Tapeout source |
| --- | --- |
| POR | vendored `por/gds/gf180mcu_ocd_ip__por.gds.gz`, unmodified |
| Bandgap | vendored `bandgap/gds/gf180mcu_ocd_ip__bandgap.gds.gz`, unmodified (a re-export from the `.mag` differs on PPLUS in the PNP cells and fails KLayout PP.2) |
| Biasgen | `biasgen_tapeout/` (GDS, LEF, `patched_sources.tar.gz`, `SHA256SUMS`), generated by the `tapeout` step from the patched Magic view with upstream's recipe (standard cells read from the Avalon GDS). Not the vendored `biasgen/gds`. |

The `fetch` step clones it into `scratch/` and mounts `pdk/libs.ref/gf180mcu_as_sc_mcu7t3v3` plus
`pdk/libs.tech/xschem` read-only at `$PDK_ROOT/gf180mcuD/libs.ref/gf180mcu_as_sc_mcu7t3v3` inside the image.

pinned revision only.
