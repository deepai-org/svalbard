# Chip-top decisions and build demands (pt_chip.conn)

Each item is a place where the two sides of a boundary disagreed or one side did not exist. `pt_chip.conn` binds
the unambiguous part and carries the D- tag in a comment. "Bound as" is what the top does today; "Proposed" is
the resolution I recommend; the owner decides. Nothing here is a scope decision.
Regression: `integration/top/run.sh` (counts in `scratch/chip-top/NOTES.md`).

## C. What the regression cannot see (after the second review, 2026-11-14)

`run.sh` checks completeness, self-consistency and what can be derived from names and device terminals. It does not see:
- **Supplies:** only pins named VDD / VDD_*_SW / vdd3v3 / DVDD and VSS-type pins are checked against the island;
  `VDDB`, `VIN`, `VISL`, bias-bank rail pins, `CASCODE` and spiral terminals are not. The island of each instance is the
  author's reading of `spec/power-delivery.md` section 2 (reviewed once by hand). States inside one enable group (one
  switch failed, `VDD_LO` off, `VREG_*`) are invisible to the crossing and audit checks.
- **Polarity and order:** derived from names. Unseen: single-ended polarity (OUTP vs OUTN), UP/DN, D/E, MAIN/POST
  crossings, the macro side of the sample buses, a constant bus offset, a wrong field split.
- **Direction:** a `>` on a gate-only pin fails; an input marker on a diffusion-only pin is only listed; `~` pins and
  the 14 boxes without a netlist (demand cells, the macro, provider pads, inline masters) are not checked.
- **Control ports:** existence, width, reset, and that an `island_on[k]` in the encoding matches the consumer's enable
  group. The decode itself is checked only for the 5 ports the boundary sim exercises.
- **Isolation audit:** the 130 macro-internal clamps and the off-state of `c_*` ports are requirements on the macro,
  shown to the audit as if present; ATB gating is read from the committed RTL when the working tree does not parse.
- `inline` pin lists other than `pt_core_bias` (compared with its builder inside the audit) are not compared with a file.
