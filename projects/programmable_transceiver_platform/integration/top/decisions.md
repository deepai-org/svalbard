# Chip-top decisions and build demands (pt_chip.conn)

Each item is a place where the two sides of a boundary disagreed or one side did not exist. `pt_chip.conn` binds
the unambiguous part and carries the D- tag in a comment. "Bound as" is what the top does today; "Proposed" is
the resolution I recommend; the owner decides. Nothing here is a scope decision.
Regression: `integration/top/run.sh` (counts in `scratch/chip-top/NOTES.md`).

## C. What the regression cannot see (review 2026-11-13)

`check_top.py` checks that the file is complete and self-consistent, not that it is right:
- direction markers (`=`, `>`, `~`) are the author's reading of each block; a wrong marker makes the driver check vacuous;
- a net with one `~` member is exempt from the undriven and crossing checks (analog crossings are only listed);
- P/N, I/Q and bit-order swaps, and which block consumes a `c_*` port, are not checked (the boundary sim covers 5 ports);
- `inline` pin lists (core_bias, iso_clamp_x4, iso_pd, the pad wrappers) are not compared with a file;
- for non-direct `cfg` rows only the existence of the field and of the macro port is checked, not the decode.
The independent review compared the bindings with the block headers by hand; its remaining findings are the D- rows above.
