# Board requirements

Obligations on the carrier and bring-up boards that the die relies on. The die is bonded on the
unmodified wafer.space chip-on-board ([package-channel.md](package-channel.md)): it carries eight 1 µF
capacitors on the supply nets and nothing on signal pins, so every signal passive below is on the carrier.
Per-application carriers are allowed: a carrier may populate only its application's passives, and a pad may
carry different functions on different carriers, but every carrier keeps bring-up, debug and fallback access.
Off-chip parts are limited to passives, the FPGA and a fallback LNA; the "BACKUP" rows are bench routes for a
partially working die, not the plan.

IDs are stable and are referenced from `performance-requirements.json`. Risk IDs are in
[risk-register.md](risk-register.md).

## Supply, reset, thermal, handling

| ID | Requirement | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **Reset supervisor IC on `RESET_N`**, open-drain, threshold inside the supply window. It holds the chip in reset on slow ramps and brownout. | The on-chip POR covers cold start and full power cycles only. It misses some 100 ms–1 s ramps and ignores dips that stay above 1.5 V. | `analog/core_support` evidence |
| B2 | **One supply source, set per die.** A trimmer plus regulator sets each die's supply at bring-up from its measured speed: 3.3 V for typical dies, up to a 3.50 V setpoint for slow dies, lower for fast dies. Setpoint + tolerance + ripple + overshoot ≤ 3.63 V. Tolerance ±1 %. Bring-up starts every die at 3.3 V. Junction temperature 22–32 °C. | Operating envelope; per-chip supply trim. | `tapeout-plan.md` |
| B2a | **Supply and thermal detail:** (1) The regulator senses at the mezzanine connector supply contacts, with ≤ 20 mΩ from the sense point to them at ≥ 0.45 A. (2) θ(junction → temperature-controlled surface) ≤ 5.2 °C/W, set point held to ±2 °C, so the junction stays within 22–32 °C across the stopped (15 mW) to full-power mode step. The connector occupies the board back side under the die, so the controlled surface is a top-side cold finger (BP-6). Free air is not acceptable. (3) A die-temperature observation for the control loop, cross-checked against an on-chip proxy such as the always-on oscillator frequency. | Drop budget 117 mV. Worst mode 1.26 W needs ≤ 6.4 °C/W; a free-air board rises by tens of °C. | `power-delivery.md` section 1, `spec/budgets/power_budget.py` |
| B52 | **Supply slew ≤ 0.3 V/µs at the die, and no hot-plug.** The carrier is never plugged into or pulled from a live supply; the regulator has a soft start. | A 1 µs supply edge fires the provider supply-pad clamps: amperes through the bonds and the die rail rings above its limit. A 10 µs edge does nothing. | `scratch/reviews/integration-m6.md` finding 1 |
| B37 | **Die supply ripple for RF modes:** the supply is LDO-post-regulated (or the switcher runs above 20 MHz or synchronized off the channel plan) with ripple ≤ 1 mV rms over 10 kHz–20 MHz at the connector contacts. | No RX supply-rejection evidence; 1–3 MHz switcher lines land inside narrowband channels. | RF-RX-PSRR |
| B49 | **`RESET_N` is host-drivable, never hard-tied high.** The B1 supervisor output is wire-ANDed with a host GPIO (open-drain or series 1 kΩ) on the same net, with one 10 kΩ pull-up to the die supply; a test point and a push-button are recommended. **With the `POR_BYPASS` strap fitted, `RESET_N` is low from before the supply starts to rise until the supply has been inside its window for ≥ 1 ms**, and then shows one clean rising edge. | `rst_n = RESET_N AND (POR OR strap OR keyed override)`: with the strap set and `RESET_N` tied high the die has no reset at all, and every island turns on during the ramp. | B50 | **SPI pins idle at the pad-pull levels whenever the host does not drive them: `SPI_CS_N` high, `SPI_SCLK` low, `SPI_MOSI` low.** Fit 1 kΩ `SPI_CS_N` to the die supply, 1 kΩ `SPI_SCLK` to ground and 1 kΩ `SPI_MOSI` to ground, or prove that the chosen FPGA leaves these pins high-impedance without pull-up from power-on through configuration. No pull-up on `SPI_MOSI` in any case. No `SPI_SCLK` edge while `SPI_CS_N` is high; a dedicated SPI bus is recommended. SPI clock ≤ 5 MHz (≤ 1 MHz at bring-up). | The pad pulls are 105–157 kΩ, weaker than any FPGA weak pull-up. `CS_N` high with `MOSI` low is the idle level that clears the pad-free POR rescue key; a floating `SCLK`/`CS_N` can clock garbage into the SPI shifter. | `rtl/pt_por_path.sv`, `rtl/pt_spi.sv`, `scratch/pads-core/pulls/` |
| B51 | **POR rescue (only for a die whose POR never releases).** First path: fit the `POR_BYPASS` jumper (strap pad to the die supply; the pad has a hard pull-down, jumper not fitted by default) and obey B49. Second path, no pad needed: the SPI key sequence of `rtl/pt_por_path.sv` ("RESCUE PROCEDURE"): the host bit-bangs `RESET_N`, `SPI_CS_N`, `SPI_SCLK`, `SPI_MOSI` as four GPIOs with ≥ 1 µs between pin changes, shifts 0xC3A5 with `RESET_N` and `CS_N` low, raises `CS_N`, then `RESET_N` with `SCLK` high, and from then on keeps `MOSI` high whenever `CS_N` is high. The B50 `MOSI` pull-down stays fitted. `RESET_STATUS` bits 4:2 report {POR masked, key override effective, strap}. Bring-up starts every die without either override and reads `RESET_STATUS` first. | The third-party POR is a single point of failure in the reset path. | `rtl/pt_por_path.sv`, `verification/check_por_path.py` |
| B26 | **Power before drive:** the supply is up and `RESET_N` released before any external source drives a chip pin: the external LO on `REF_IN`/`AUX_CLK` (B3), link partners on `WIRE_RX`/`WTX_EXT`/`AUX_CLK`, TMDS pull-ups (B9), `BB_I/Q` injection and ATB force (B7). Remove them in reverse order. The FPGA gates the LO source and the B6 switch on a power-good signal. | Externally driven pads land on p-diffusion in islands that may be unpowered: latch-up and back-powering (LU-02, LU-03). | risk-register LU-02, LU-03 |
| B6 | **Host lines are high-impedance while the chip is unpowered.** Use a bus switch with powered-off isolation on the 11 D2H lines and `SPI_MISO`. Never leave the chip supply floating while the FPGA is powered. | The provider host pads backfeed about 6.8 mA per pin into the ring. Series resistors are acceptable only on `SPI_MISO`. | `analog/pads` powered-host-backfeed |
| B6a | **The B6 switch must fit the host-link load budget:** on-capacitance ≤ 3 pF per line, bandwidth ≥ 1 GHz, propagation ≤ 0.3 ns, channel-to-channel skew ≤ 50 ps, isolation with its supply at 0 V. | ≤ 10 pF total per line (HL-B3). | HL-B3 |
| B7 | **Analog test bus force (`ATB0`/`ATB1`) only while the chip is powered**, or with the source limited to 1 mA. | Unpowered, a 50 Ω source pushes 54–56 mA into the ring through the provider analog pad. | `analog/pads` atb-provider-pad-force |
| B10 | **Access to every debug pad** (`BB_I/Q`, `AUX_CLK`, `WTX_EXT`, `WMON`, `CLK_OBS`, `DBG_OBS0/1`, `ATB0/1`, `TLP_TEST`, `POR_BYPASS`): an SMA or probe point each. `DBG_OBS0/1` and `CLK_OBS` also go to FPGA inputs (HL-B8). | Fallback routes and observability on every carrier. | `tapeout-plan.md` |
| B28 | **Opaque cover:** black glob-top or an opaque lid over the die for every measurement; microscope lamps off. | Photocurrent: about 0.3 µA under room light against 10 µA bias, about 100× more under a microscope lamp (OTH-01). The glob-top colour is not confirmed. | risk-register OTH-01 |
| B48 | **ESD handling:** (1) all handling, assembly and bring-up in an EPA per ANSI/ESD S20.20 or IEC 61340-5-1; (2) wrist strap and grounded mat for every bench probe; ground the probe and the coax shield before touching RF or high-speed pins; (3) discharge cables before connecting them; (4) the pin list states the HBM level per pin (2 kV: supplies, host link, debug/ATB, RF_RX, clocks, USB, WIRE_RX; 1 kV: RF_TX, WIRE_TX) and states no CDM rating (≥ 250 V is a target, not verified). | The die is an unencapsulated chip-on-board with mixed ESD levels. | `scratch/reviews/esd-tiering.md` |

The die attach (conductive, grounded paddle) is wafer.space's choice and is not confirmed in writing; substrate
isolation figures that assume a grounded back side depend on it (SUB-10).

## Package and carrier

| ID | Requirement | Why |
|---|---|---|
| BP-1 | **Package model = per-pin chain** of `package-channel.json`: bond wire (per-bond length, inductance and resistance), 86.6 Ω microstrip, one via, connector. Every RF, LO and multi-Gb/s simulation that claims a board requirement includes it. | No signal passive can sit closer than the carrier. |
| BP-2 | **Carrier length compensation.** Add carrier length to the shorter leg of each pair (5.9 ps/mm on the board): BB_Q P +2.5 mm, WTX_EXT P +2.1, WIRE_TX N +2.0, BB_I P +1.7, RF_RX P +1.0 (board-equivalent mm). | BP-3 | **RF and LO passives within 10 mm of the connector** (≤ 30 mm at about +0.8 dB). 50 Ω coplanar-grounded lines with ground vias at each connector ground contact. RF_RX at contacts 12/13, LO Q 20/21, LO I 22/23, RF_TX 26/27. | Loss grows from 0.4 to 2.6 dB between 2 and 50 mm. |
| BP-4 | **The eight board capacitors (1 µF) populated**, plus ≥ 1 µF + 100 nF per supply contact within 5 mm on the carrier and ≥ 10 µF bulk. | They act below about 30 MHz; without them every supply figure is 1.2–1.5 × worse. |

## Host link (FPGA to chip)

Ten data bits plus a forwarded clock in each direction, DDR, 312.5 Mb/s per pin (156.25 MHz clock) at full
rate, 3.3 V LVCMOS at the die supply. D2H is on the south edge, H2D on the north edge; directions are fixed
in the current RTL. The timing budget is in [interface-requirements.md](interface-requirements.md) (HL-*);
the FPGA screen is in [fpga-connection-plan.md](fpga-connection-plan.md).

| ID | Requirement | Why |
|---|---|---|
| HL-B1 | **Topology.** D2H and H2D are separate source-synchronous groups, each clock routed with its data. Clocks go to clock-capable FPGA pins; H2D_CLK is forwarded from an output DDR register, centred in the data eye. A board that wants reversible banks later (HL-DIR) routes both clocks to pins that are clock-capable and bidirectional. | The chip captures H2D on H2D_CLK and launches D2H edge-aligned with D2H_CLK. |
| HL-B2 | **Impedance and length.** 50 Ω ±10 % single-ended over a continuous ground plane. Data-to-clock length matched within ±2.5 mm. Total length ≤ 60 mm. Series source termination 22–33 Ω within 5 mm of each driver: D2H at the connector, H2D at the FPGA. | Edges are 0.76–1.4 ns: the lines are transmission lines. |
| HL-B3 | **Load.** ≤ 10 pF per line, counting FPGA pin, trace and the B6 switch. | Chip-side screens used 6–10 pF. |
| HL-B4 | **Simultaneous switching.** All 11 D2H outputs may switch on one edge: one ground via per 2 host signals within 3 mm of the connector; ≥ 2 × 100 nF plus 1 µF on each host supply contact within 5 mm; ground bounce ≤ 300 mV at a quiet D2H pin (HL-SSO). The FPGA respects its vendor limit for 11 LVCMOS33 outputs in one bank. | Package model: 486 mV at 0.76 ns edges unstaggered. |
| HL-B5 | **Receiver window and training.** Per-bit input delay (≤ 80 ps step, ≥ 1.6 ns range) and per-lane word alignment, trained on the chip's training prefix. FPGA input setup + hold ≤ 1.0 ns at the pin. | The D2H budget assumes a 1000 ps FPGA window. |
| HL-B6 | **Rate modes.** Bring-up starts at `debug.host_link.rate` = div4 (78.125 Mb/s per pin). RF modes run at div2 (PWR-HOST-RF). Full rate only after training passes. | Reduced-rate fallback; host IO current. |
| HL-B7 | **No FPGA pulls or keepers on the host banks** except the B50 SPI resistors. If reversible banks (HL-DIR) are built, the gateware tristates a bank before the chip is told to drive it. | Contention would be 30–40 mA per pin. |
| HL-B8 | **Observation pins to the FPGA.** `DBG_OBS0`, `DBG_OBS1` and `CLK_OBS` go to 3.3 V FPGA inputs without pulls; `CLK_OBS` on a clock-capable input. Test-point stubs ≤ 5 mm. | Status, events and frequency measurement without an in-band frame. |
