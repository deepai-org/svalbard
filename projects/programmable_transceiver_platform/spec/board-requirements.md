# Board requirements from the tapeout work

These are obligations on the bring-up and product boards that the die relies
on. Each was created by a design decision or a silicon limitation recorded in
`tapeout-readiness.json`. The external-component allowance in the
[pin plan](../../../docs/roadmap/programmable-transceiver-pin-plan.md) permits
all of them.

## Host link (FPGA ↔ chip) board requirements

The link is 10 data bits plus a forwarded clock in each direction, DDR. It runs at 312.5 Mb/s per pin (156.25 MHz clock) at full rate, and at 2.5 Gb/s line rate under the divider-8 rule (interface-architecture-changes.md AC-1). All levels are 3.3 V LVCMOS: the chip has no separate host-IO supply. The timing budget is in [interface-requirements.md](interface-requirements.md) (HL-*).

| ID | Requirement | Why |
|---|---|---|
| HL-B1 | **Topology.** D2H (south COB edge) and H2D (north edge) are separate source-synchronous groups. Each group's clock routes alongside its data. Clocks go to clock-capable FPGA inputs (regional/IO clock buffers) or come from an ODDR-forwarded output, with H2D_CLK centred in the data eye. **Reversible banks (HL-DIR):** route **both** H2D_CLK and D2H_CLK to FPGA pins that are clock-capable **and** bidirectional (Artix-7/Spartan-7 MRCC/SRCC; ECP5 PCLK). On Cyclone V the dedicated CLK inputs are input-only, so use a bidirectional DQS/regular pin and training (verify), or forgo RX2/TX2. | The chip captures H2D on H2D_CLK and launches D2H centred on D2H_CLK. |
| HL-B2 | **Impedance and length.** 50 Ω ±10 % single-ended over a continuous ground plane, with no plane split under either group.
