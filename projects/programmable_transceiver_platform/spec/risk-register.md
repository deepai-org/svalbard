# Transceiver risk register

The single risk register for the GF180 programmable transceiver. Other documents cite these IDs and keep no risk lists of their own.

**220 risks:** open 133, demanded-mitigation 61, risk-accepted 11, mitigated 9, closed 6.

**Status:** *open* = unresolved or analysis pending; *demanded-mitigation* = a fix is demanded and not yet proven; *risk-accepted* = the residual is accepted; *mitigated* / *closed* = kept only because the row records a rule that must keep being obeyed. **Score** = likelihood × impact (each 1–5). Rows are sorted open and demanded first by score, then accepted, mitigated, closed.

To add a risk: append a row with the next free number in its prefix, a score, status, mitigation/owner and one evidence path (`scratch/` paths are relative to the repository root, others to this project). Change status in place; delete a row when its fix is fully embodied in the design and leaves no standing rule. IDs are never reused; *aka* lists merged IDs.
