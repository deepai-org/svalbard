# Transceiver risk register

**This is the single canonical risk register for the GF180 programmable transceiver.** All new risks go here, as a row in the table below, and nowhere else (rule in `scratch/reviews/MANDATE.md`). Other documents link here instead of keeping their own risk lists.

Consolidated 2026-11-11 from: the old `scratch/reviews/risk-register.md`/`.json` (all 92 entries, IDs kept), `spec/risk-priorities.md` (open-issue table), `risks.yaml`, `scratch/reviews/interface-changes.md` (RA-*, PD*, accepted items), `performance-review.md/json`, `rx-robustness-review.md`, `physical-limits*`, `consolidation-pass-3.md`, `host-link-bandwidth.md`, `spec/tapeout-plan.md`, `block-deliverables.md`, `package-channel.md`, `board-requirements.md`, and the block READMEs. Duplicates were merged; merged-away and source IDs are listed under *aka*. The full per-risk analysis text of the 92 legacy entries (mechanism, magnitude, analysis results) is kept read-only (superseded, not maintained) in the local `scratch/reviews/archive/risk-register-legacy-2026-11-11.md`/`.json`; numbers here are the newest found.

**223 risks:** open 102, demanded-mitigation 66, risk-accepted 27, mitigated 12, closed 16.

**Status:** *open* = unresolved or analysis pending; *demanded-mitigation* = an analysis demands a fix that is not yet proven; *mitigated* = fix applied with evidence; *risk-accepted* = coordinator accepted the residual; *closed* = not needed / retired. **Score** = likelihood × impact (each 1–5). Rows are sorted open/demanded first, then by score.

To add a risk: append a row with the next free ID in its prefix (WRX, WTX, RFR, RFT, CNV, PAD, DIG, TIM, LO, HL, INT, BRD, PWR, MOD, SUB, PDN, XT, BW, SP, IQ, TH, LU, ESD, …), a score, status, mitigation/owner and an evidence path. Change status in place; put a dated note in the Notes section only if needed.
