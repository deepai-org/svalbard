# Integration-risk plan (chip level), 2026-11-13

Scope: failures that block-level evidence can't see. Inputs: the v6 outline assembly
(`scratch/chip-assembly-v6/run/report-v6.json`, image `docs/images/chip-assembly-2026-11-13-v6.png`),
`spec/risk-register.md`, the A1–A8/TXC/PKG analyses (`scratch/reviews/physical-limits/analyses/RESULTS.md`),
`spec/tapeout-plan.md` Tier 1, `spec/package-channel.json`, and `rtl/pt_digital_macro.sv`.

Status words: **exists** = a check has been run; **partial** = run on an older floorplan or a subset; **none**.

Goal: power up safely, read the SPI ID, walk the ATB, see DBG_OBS, with every ambitious block dead.

## 3. Ranking

Ranked by severity × likelihood ÷ cost. The die-1 items come first.

1. **G3: POR override.** A dead die with nothing to debug. Zero-to-small area; RTL plus one pad or strap. *Decide now.*
2. **M6: power-up transient and isolation-state audit** (covers G1, G5, G8): small netlists, a script plus about 6 short sims.
3. **M1: top-level PDN RC model with block current sources.** Covers PDN-02/03/05/08, G2 and the 0-nF decap outcome of v6. The model also feeds M2/M3/M4.
4. **M2: substrate macro-model on v6 distances** (wrx_cap and QVCO added). Extends A1.
5. **M3: two-ring + QVCO pulling co-sim** (shared pt_pll_vreg2 never simulated with both rings).
6. **M7: bias/reference fault injection.** DC only, cheap.
7. **M4/M5: per-pin package + full-ring ESD on the assembled rails.** Needed by tapeout; depends on the bond map.
8. **M8 thermal, M9 antenna, M10 congestion.** On the routed v6/v7.

Blocking all of these: **the chip doesn't fit** (v6 raw margin −1.42 mm²; −2.3 mm² with a 10 % routing allowance, −3.0 mm² adding 3 nF of stacked decap). That is INT-03 and needs a coordinator decision before any top-level check runs on a real layout.

## 4. First three checks to run

1. **POR / isolation / power-up (M6 + G3).** Extracted POR, biasgen, aosc, the isw switches and two pad families on a lumped PDN (bond R/L from package-channel.json). Ramps of 10 µs, 1 ms and 1 s; overshoot to 3.63 V; a brownout dip to 2.0 V. Pass criteria: por_n releases on every ramp, or the override reaches the SPI flops; every island stays off; total always-on current ≤ budget at ff/85/3.63. Must-fail cases: a POR stuck low with no override, and an inverted ISO. Also run the isolation-state script over the chip netlist.
2. **Top-level PDN model (M1).** The R mesh from the v6 strap plan, per-pin bonds, decap as placed (0 nF in v6) and as demanded (3–4 nF stacked MOS+MIM), and block current sources from the evidence files. Outputs: IR at each tap, the bounce spectrum at core_bias/ADC/QVCO taps, and the anti-resonance Q. This turns the decap demand into an area number for the v7 floorplan.
3. **Substrate + pulling (M2 + M3, one bench).** The A1 mesh re-reduced on v6 distances, driven by the M1 bounce at the digital, wrx_cap and ring taps. Victims: the extracted ADC comparator input, the QVCO tank, and both rings on the shared pt_pll_vreg2. Pass criteria: SUB-01 ADC spur limit, QVCO spur ≤ −80 dBc (LO20), ring pulling ≤ the CDR KP range.
